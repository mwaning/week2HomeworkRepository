Cacturne

Cacturne is a Grass/Dark type pokemon introduced in Generation 3. It is based on a cactus, as well as a scarecrow. It is a nocturnal pokemon that does not move during the day to preserve energy and hunts for prey that are worn out from the desert heat. Cacturne move in packs, often following groups of travelers through the desert, waiting until they cannot move.
